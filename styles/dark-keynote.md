# Dark tech keynote

**Good for:** one big result, a launch-style hook, the headline number of the user's project.

## Look

- Near-black `#07080b`, text `#f5f5f7`, secondary text `#86868b`, one accent `#5e9bff` on one thing only.
- One giant number at 30% of the frame height (`Inter` 700, about 320 px).

## Motion

- Easing `bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (`E.out`), 30 to 45 frames per move.
- Light sweeps reveal objects: a soft white band moving across, under a mask of the object.

## Avoid

More than one accent use, busy backgrounds, fast cuts, small text.

## Build

HTML text and a canvas for sweeps. Use it for one or two scenes, not a whole teaching video.
