Kaleidoscope

A skill for Claude Code29 animation styles · 6 reviewer agents · 2 review rounds

Kaleidoscope makes a narrated video that teaches one topic. Each part of the video has its own animation style.

Claude writes the script, the storyboard and the scenes from what your conversation and your project already show. The engine speaks the script on your own GPU, times each word and draws every frame with code. It uses no video model. Six reviewer agents then check the video in two rounds before it renders.

In Claude Code, in the conversation where the topic came up, ask:

Make a video on I2C for the BNO085, and tell its history as a 1920s newsreel.

How a video is made

Claude plans and builds. The engine speaks, times and draws. The reviewers send fixes back to Claude.

→ SKILL.md

  1. script.md
    storyboard.md
    scenes.jsClaude writes    One part for each idea, one style for each part, one scene for each part. Claude checks every fact before it writes the script.
  2. Chatterboxvoice, on your GPUSpeaks the script.
  3. Whisperword timingGives each word and each [#cue] a time in seconds. If a sentence does not match the script, the engine speaks it again.
  4. headless ChromeframesDraws each frame from the timeline with canvas, Three.js or Manim.
  5. make checkautomatic checksTests overlap, the safe area, text size and pacing. Claude also looks at every changed part, frame by frame.
  6. 6 reviewer agentstwo roundsRead the review pack and send one fix list back. Claude fixes the scenes and builds again.
  7. make finalrenderChrome draws on the GPU and NVENC encodes. verify checks the duration, the loudness and black frames. The result is an MP4 with soft subtitles and 48 kHz sound.

→ agents/

Before the reviewers start, Claude reads contact sheets like this one. Then six agents check the video. A seventh agent joins their reports into one short fix list. Claude fixes the scenes and builds again, and the second round checks the fixes.

  • video-factcheckEvery fact, number and formula, worked out again.
  • video-learnerA first-time viewer answers a quiz and says where it got lost.
  • video-alignmentThe picture shows what the voice says, at that moment.
  • video-overlapNo text or drawing covers another.
  • video-physicsMotion: jumps, pops, easing and weight.
  • video-overallLayout, legibility, colour meaning and pacing.
  • video-compilerJoins the six reports into one short fix list.

Music made from code

The music plays about 16 dB under the voice and ducks gently when the voice speaks.

→ SOUND.md

The engine makes its background music in engine/music.mjs. It uses no samples, no AI models and no recordings, so there is no licence to clear. The same track and length always give the same file. These are 30 s previews of the two approved tracks.

  • current72 BPM · C Am F G

    A calm pad and a slow arpeggio, with no beat. This is the default track, and it suits any video.

  • lofi76 BPM · swing

    A lo-fi study beat: electric piano, bass, soft drums and vinyl hiss. Use it for a calm video that needs more energy.

The 29 styles

Claude picks a style for each part from the type of idea in it. Two parts in a row never use the same style.

→ styles/README.md

Maths and diagrams 7

  • math-manimThe 3Blue1Brown look, drawn by Manim. The plane moves and formulas morph.
  • signal-graphNodes and edges. Pulses travel along the edges, and edge width shows weight.
  • pixel-gridA zoomed-in pixel grid with values. A kernel window slides over it.
  • single-lineOne line draws everything at a steady speed and never lifts.
  • shape-morphNo cuts. Each idea grows out of the previous shape.
  • whiteboardaDry-erase marker on a glossy board, written stroke by stroke.
  • blueprintaWhite lines on Prussian blue, drawn in drafting order with dimensions.

Characters and stories 7

  • flat-vectorFlat shapes with overshoot entrances. A growing shape changes the scene.
  • line-boilHand-drawn ink that boils, animated on twos. Faces act.
  • paper-cutoutCard layers with cut edges, stop motion and a parallax camera.
  • pixel-artA 320 × 180 canvas, a fixed palette, dithering and typed dialogue.
  • midcentury-toona1950s flat colour off a broken ink line. A presenter's hands do each step.
  • watercolourdBrush strokes paint themselves in, and washes bleed past the ink.
  • risographdTwo or three inks that multiply, with halftone dots and offset plates.

3D and space 6

  • isometricA miniature world of three-tone blocks. Roofs lift off to show the inside.
  • iso-infographicaA floating board with a route, label pins and counts that add up to a total.
  • toy-3dSoft pastel Three.js objects that land with weight and a contact shadow.
  • teardownaA real product in Three.js from its CAD files. Its layers lift apart.
  • desktop-flythroughaApps as paper cards on a sunlit desk. Voxel toys hop between them.
  • transit-mapaA metro map on paper. Lines grow from pen tips, and interchanges fall into place.

Data and evidence 9

  • data-storydOne chart per screen, drawn mark by mark by a pencil.
  • data-physicalaNumbers built as stacks of metal, acrylic and brass on a wooden base.
  • briefing-boardaAn enamel board with photos and magnets. One red stamp gives the verdict.
  • telestratoraFootage or a photo, frozen and drawn on with rings and marker arrows.
  • swiss-typeA 12-column grid, kinetic type, hard snaps and one signal colour.
  • dark-keynoteNear-black, one giant number and slow light sweeps.
  • hud-terminalMonospace on a character grid. Numbers roll and brackets lock on.
  • film-crtOne era of film or tape, with grain, gate weave and chroma shift.
  • newsreelaA 1920s newsreel in a silver film gate, with place cards, an iris and wipes.

a We adapted this style from Lemo-Opuscar by LemoLab (MIT). d We took details for this style from Lemo-Opuscar.

Install

Clone the repo into your skills folder, then run doctor. It shows what is missing.

→ INSTALL.md

git clone git@github.com:jjietay/kaleidoscope.git ~/.claude/skills/kaleidoscope
cd ~/.claude/skills/kaleidoscope
node engine/make.mjs doctor      # shows what is missing

INSTALL.md has the full setup for the voice, the word timing, Manim and the reviewer agents. Then ask for a video in the conversation where the topic came up:

  • Make a video explaining how the EKF fuses wheel odometry and the IMU.
  • Make a video on I2C for the BNO085, and tell its history as a 1920s newsreel.
