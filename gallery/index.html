Kaleidoscope

A Claude Code skill that makes a narrated teaching video about a topic. Every part of the video has its own animation style, and six reviewer agents check the video before it renders.

29 animation styles Code draws every frame 6 reviewer agents Voice on your own GPU

How a video is made

Claude writes the script, the storyboard and the scenes. The engine makes the voice, times each word and draws the frames. Two rounds of reviewers then send fixes back to Claude.
